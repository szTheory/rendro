## Trust Boundaries

| Observer → committed/package evidence | Public metadata becomes a durable shipped artifact. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-131-01 | Tampering | `scripts/adoption_snapshot.exs` | high | mitigate | Strict enums/types, canonical digest, exact query/limit, atomic exclusive output, and offline recomputation of family/composite decisions. |
| T-131-02 | Information Disclosure | evidence sidecar | high | mitigate | Key allowlist and negative fixtures prohibit bodies, headers, tokens, env, private reports, and local paths. |
| T-131-03 | Repudiation | ADOPTION projection | medium | mitigate | Date/source/outcome/digest and exact sidecar link bind public prose to machine facts. |
| T-131-04 | Denial of Service | public retrieval | low | accept | One bounded invocation records UNAVAILABLE and exits; there is no polling/retry daemon. |
</threat_model>
