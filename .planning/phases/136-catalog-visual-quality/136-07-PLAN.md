3. Boundary predicate: candidate classification is exactly six changed IDs and 26 controls, never a subset, superset, or family match.

7. Adjacency predicate: bundle validation immediately precedes image eligibility; no image is interpreted between download and validation.
8. Adjacency predicate: each review image identity remains adjacent to its exact candidate SHA/run/renderer/PDF/PNG binding.
9. Adjacency predicate: canonical dispatch remains downstream of all six complete threshold results, never merely downstream of generation.

11. Empty predicate: zero bundle artifacts yields no review authority and no fallback to historical evidence.

13. Ordering predicate: relevant review images retain locked Invoice light/dark, Statement light/dark, Payslip light/dark, Ticket light/dark order.
