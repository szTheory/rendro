<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Change reach and public-behavior boundary

- **D-01 — Strict hybrid boundary:** preserve `catalog_layout: true` only for demonstrated fixture-capacity geometry, such as the existing Statement header and Ticket band accommodations. Hierarchy, typography, semantic ink, rules, labels, and color behavior belong to each recipe's normal supplied-theme path. — **Reversibility: costly** — catalog previews, public themed recipe output, deterministic hashes, quality bindings, and downstream Phase-131 evidence all consume this boundary.
- **D-02 — Catalog output must remain truthful public output:** do not create a marketing-quality rendering path that looks better than an equivalent public `Recipe.document(data, theme: ...)` call. A catalog fixture may need extra capacity, but it may not receive better visual semantics.
- **D-03 — No identity or genre branches in recipes:** visual behavior must not branch on catalog ID, brand, preset, quality status, or individual flagship cell. Recipes consume semantic theme roles; presets vary token values behind those roles.
- **D-04 — Preserve frozen and evolving contracts:** keep every no-theme byte identity intact. Supplied-theme token values and rendered bytes may evolve through the documented Evolving tier, but changes require targeted semantic contracts and refreshed evidence.
- **D-05 — No new consumer burden:** add no public option, callback, behavior, dependency, or configuration knob for this repair. The existing recipe/theme contract must become more correct without exposing catalog mechanics.

### Cross-genre hierarchy treatment

- **D-06 — One semantic focal fact per family:** every repaired render has exactly one dominant fact or tightly bound fact-group. Typography carries the primary emphasis; proximity, whitespace, rules, and restrained surfaces only clarify the relationship and reading order. Color reinforces hierarchy but never carries it alone.
- **D-07 — Corporate-Classic Invoice:** create a compact payment-summary relationship in which `Total Due` is unambiguously dominant and the due date is adjacent but subordinate. Retain the subtotal/tax/total arithmetic ladder and the formal Corporate-Classic character.
- **D-08 — Minimal-Mono Statement:** retain the austere full-width closing-balance band, enlarge or better isolate the mono closing amount through measured whitespace and rule contrast, and avoid generic card or dashboard styling.
- **D-09 — Humanist Receipt hierarchy:** retain a type-led `Total` as the sole display-scale fact, separate the arithmetic ladder from the item table with a quiet rule/whitespace break, and avoid a large panel on the narrow receipt.
- **D-10 — Editorial Certificate:** make the recipient the largest ceremonial fact, the credential clearly second-ranked and nearby, and the issuer/body/signatory quiet. Use centered whitespace and editorial typography; do not add business-form bands, boxes, or summary cards.
- **D-11 — Swiss Payslip:** make `Net Pay` a sharp grid-aligned summary band with a dominant atomic amount and subordinate label, followed by denser earnings/deductions reconciliation tables. Preserve right alignment and indivisible money values.
- **D-12 — Brutalist Ticket:** make the placement grid the bounded visual anchor through scale plus a hard-edged grouping or rule. Keep the event title secondary and the complete reference compact, legible, and subordinate; do not repeat the Phase-126 reference-as-display inversion.
- **D-13 — Preserve genre and geometry:** do not flatten the six presets into one visual motif. Keep light/dark hierarchy rank and geometry equivalent, Certificate landscape, Ticket native A6, the existing pagination model, and the current fixed family page geometry.

### Humanist dark Receipt repair

- **D-14 — Repair the actual semantic-color defect:** themed Receipt table headers, descriptions, and amounts use semantic `ink`; the page number and genuinely secondary labels use semantic `muted`. Measurement and rendering must use the same materialized styles.
- **D-15 — Keep the warm dark identity:** retain the warm-neutral dark page. Do not lighten the full page or table surface to conceal black text, and do not use the Poppy & Grain accent as routine or essential foreground text.
- **D-16 — Add one restrained arithmetic treatment:** use a subtle semantic `surface` plus `rule` treatment around the subtotal/tax/total sequence. It should read as warm paper translated into dark mode, not as a rounded SaaS card, and the `Total` remains the sole display-scale element.
- **D-17 — Bound the repair:** introduce no new theme role, public token, preset, fixture, font, recipe, catalog cell, or layout API. The light sibling must remain coherent, all no-theme Receipt bytes remain frozen, and any wider supplied-theme Receipt change receives explicit regression evidence.
- **D-18 — Accessibility is a lens, not a claim:** inspect foreground/background pairing, hierarchy without color, full-size legibility, and grayscale resilience, but retain the screen-oriented, non-print-safe disclosure and make no WCAG, PDF/UA, accessibility, universal-viewer, or universal-design assertion.

### Review cadence, promotion, and evidence closure

- **D-19 — Staged engineering, single human authority:** after each family-pair repair, run focused deterministic and semantic regression checks only. Interim checkpoints are diagnostic and non-promotional: do not edit `rubric_scores.json`, `SIGN-OFF.md`, a `passed` value, or a public quality projection.
- **D-20 — Regenerate the fixed catalog once the six pairs stabilize:** generate and check the complete ordered 32-cell catalog. Any mechanically changed unscored cell receives an explicit hash/reason rebind without being scored; never mass-rebless, invent quality evidence, or silently retain a stale binding.
- **D-21 — One final pinned advisory payload:** after deterministic stability, produce one pinned-PDFium payload containing the exact twelve full-size page-one review images in canonical family order, light immediately followed by dark, plus renderer version/SHA and current catalog PNG/source-PDF hashes. Required deterministic CI and pinned-raster advisory evidence remain separate.
- **D-22 — Review evidence, not expected outcomes:** the final human review records observed scores and concise bounded justifications for each exact artifact. A contact sheet may index the set but cannot replace full-size sequential inspection.
- **D-23 — Promotion remains threshold-derived:** a light cell may become `passes` only when `content_hierarchy == 5`, every other core dimension is `>= 4`, both gates are true, hashes are current, and signer/date plus `supersedes_evidence_ref` and `resolution_ref` are concrete. No quota or desired green count may alter a score.
- **D-24 — Dark cells remain truthful `needs_work`:** dark cells retain `print_safety: false`; under the current unchanged arithmetic they remain `needs_work` even when their on-screen hierarchy, reader affordances, typographic craft, or cohesion improve. Humanist dark Receipt must repair its recorded deficits without being promoted merely for improvement.
- **D-25 — Stop fail-closed:** stop on any hash mismatch, missing/changed pair, deterministic contract failure, unavailable or mismatched pinned renderer, stale disposition, or missed threshold. Re-run only the affected engineering checkpoint and final payload; never treat unavailable evidence as success or as zero.
- **D-26 — Evidence microcopy:** distinguish `Engineering checkpoint — not a human-quality disposition.`, `Candidate catalog state — deterministic checks passed; advisory review pending.`, `Final human review — inspect this exact light/dark pair; record observed evidence, not an assumed pass.`, and `Disposition bound to current artifact identity.`

### the agent's Discretion

The planner may choose private helper names, exact numeric type/spacing/rule adjustments, the internal representation of a Receipt arithmetic backdrop, focused test-module placement, and the smallest deterministic checkpoint command per family. It may decide which shared semantic helper to reuse after inspecting measurement and render paths. It may not move visual policy behind `catalog_layout`, branch by preset/brand/catalog identity, change the rubric arithmetic, promote a dark cell by changing its print boundary, expose a new public API, or merge deterministic and advisory authority.

</decisions>

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets

- `Rendro.Theme` already materializes semantic `ink`, `muted`, `background`, `surface`, `rule`, and accent roles for light/dark without draw-time color math.
- Every recipe already separates the frozen no-theme path from a supplied-theme `palette/1` and `typography/1` path, so visual repairs can remain additive to themed behavior.
- Statement and Payslip already demonstrate summary-band treatment; Receipt can reuse the semantic pattern without adding a new product surface.
- Phase 126 introduced or established shared semantic table-cell behavior and protected atomic money/hierarchy contracts that Phase 130 should reuse rather than bypass.
- `Rendro.Catalog` already enforces exact IDs/order/count, complete-PDF and PNG hashes, derived quality projections, and closure references for a passing transition.

### Established Patterns

- Recipes own document semantics; presets supply genre token values; catalog tooling composes both but does not own visual policy.
- Unthemed bytes are frozen. Supplied-theme values may evolve only with explicit deterministic and advisory evidence.
- Generation writes artifacts and derived manifests, never human judgments. Reviewer records remain a separate exact one-to-one relation.
- Deterministic checks are merge authority; pinned PDFium and human review are advisory evidence with bounded claims.
- Review occurs full-size, sequentially, family by family, with light immediately followed by dark.

### Integration Points

- Six recipe-local hierarchy seams produce the twelve scored cells and may mechanically affect other themed catalog cells; the full catalog/hash relation must therefore be regenerated and deliberately rebound.
- Receipt table measurement/render construction, footer page numbering, and totals composition jointly own the Humanist dark repair.
- `rubric_scores.json`, `SIGN-OFF.md`, catalog manifest quality projection, and the required checker consume the final human dispositions.
- The isolated raster CI route produces review inputs; it must not become required deterministic authority.

</code_context>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Treat each document like its domain, not a generic UI card: Corporate-Classic payment summary, Minimal-Mono closing band, Humanist arithmetic ladder, Editorial ceremony, Swiss payroll grid, and Brutalist placement block.
- The Receipt dark page stays warm-neutral. Essential text uses semantic foreground roles; accent color is decoration or emphasis only.
- Engineering checkpoints explicitly say they are not dispositions. The final reviewer is shown exact hash-bound light/dark pairs and is asked to record observed evidence rather than an assumed pass.
- A successful repair is allowed to remain `needs_work`; honest current evidence is the product outcome.
