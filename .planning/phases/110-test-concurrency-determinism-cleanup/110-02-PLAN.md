Purpose: Nondeterministic tests must not block PRs via blind retries. Additionally, we must record our architectural decisions against test partitioning and document why certain tests remain `async: false`.
Output: Updated `mix.exs` aliases, a quarantined `RecipesFacadeDriftTest`, and documented testing guidelines in `test_helper.exs`.
