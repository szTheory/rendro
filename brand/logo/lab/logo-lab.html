Phase 103 of milestone B1. Five integrated typemark directions, each judged against the same constraints. Pick one (or ask to iterate) — the chosen direction becomes the full lockup system in Phase 104.

A · Frame-R option-a-frame-r

The capital R is the mark: its bowl is a page frame with a folded corner (pagination), and its leg kicks past the baseline — content leaving the page as finished output. The mark is the first letter, so the wordmark is inherently unified, no separate icon.

B · Page-o option-b-page-o

Lowercase, monospaced — the package register (rendro as you type it in mix.exs). The final o becomes two overlapping pages: the pagination motif, worked into the counter rather than bolted on.

C · Flow-line option-c-flowline

Content lines flow into the wordmark; a single rule threads beneath it and lifts off to the right into a page — data → document rendered literally in the lockup. The flow line breaks the boundary upward (no box, deliberately open).
